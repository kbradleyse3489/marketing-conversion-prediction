Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/222/bank+marketing.zip

Retrieved October 17, 2026. CSV unchanged. UCI's page covers multiple versions; this project specifically uses 41,188-row additional-full, not the 45,211-row older dataset. Metrics, figures and scores are derived analysis. No endorsement implied.

The seven-input subset is an analytical choice inherited from the uploaded script. The source dataset's full 20 inputs remain in the CSV. No current private customer data is included. UCI documents the rows as date-ordered May 2008 through November 2010; complete dates and customer IDs are not supplied.
